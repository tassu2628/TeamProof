ABOUT TEAMPROOF

Building Fairer Teams, One Contribution at a Time.

TeamProof is designed to bring transparency, accountability, and fairness to college group projects. We help teams organize tasks and make individual contributions more visible.

OUR MISSION

Every Student Deserves Fair Recognition.

In group projects, it can be difficult to identify how much each student has contributed. TeamProof aims to solve this problem by maintaining task records, collecting work evidence, and tracking project activity.

Our goal is to encourage better teamwork and help faculty review individual contributions more clearly.
