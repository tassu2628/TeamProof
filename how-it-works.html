HOW IT WORKS

From Teamwork to Transparent Contributions.

TeamProof makes group project management easier with a simple process for assigning tasks, submitting evidence, and reviewing contributions.

FOUR SIMPLE STEPS

Your Project Journey, Simplified.

Follow these steps to organize your project and keep everyone's work visible.

STEP 01
📁

Create a Project

Start a project by adding its title, description, deadline, and team details.

STEP 02
📋

Assign Team Tasks

Divide project work into tasks, assign team members, and set deadlines.

STEP 03
📤

Submit Work Evidence

Team members submit completed work using project links and supporting files.

STEP 04
🛡️

Review Contributions

Faculty reviews submitted evidence and checks individual task contributions.

THE RESULT

Better Records. Better Teamwork.

By maintaining clear task records and work evidence, TeamProof aims to make project progress easier to understand and individual contributions easier to review.
