THE CHALLENGE

Is Every Team Member Contributing Equally?

Group projects become challenging when individual contributions aren't clearly tracked and responsibilities aren't transparent.

⚖

Unequal Contributions

Some team members do more work while others contribute less, making fair recognition difficult.

👁

Lack of Transparency

Without proper records, it's difficult to identify who completed which tasks.

📋

Unclear Responsibilities

Missing task records and unclear ownership can make teamwork and accountability difficult.
